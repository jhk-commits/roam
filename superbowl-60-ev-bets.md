## Methodology

**Sharp reference lines** are derived from Circa Sports (known sharp-friendly Vegas book) and market consensus from professional-facing sportsbooks. Pinnacle and Bookmaker lines were not directly available via web scraping, but Circa's lines serve the same purpose: they accept large sharp action and their prices reflect efficient market odds.

**+EV is identified when:** FanDuel or DraftKings offer a line/price that is more generous than the sharp consensus, meaning the implied probability you're paying for is *lower* than the true probability suggested by the efficient market.

## +EV Bets (Ranked by Confidence)

### TIER 1: Strongest +EV Plays

#### 1. Patriots +4.5 (-105) at DraftKings/BetMGM
**Why it's +EV:**
- Circa (sharp book) has this line with nearly even money on both sides, and sharp dollar flow is balanced (52% SEA). At DraftKings, 64% of the handle is on Seattle, pushing juice toward the public side.
- Underdogs have covered in **5 consecutive Super Bowls** (longest streak ever) and 16 of the last 23.
- Sharp bettors at Circa are nearly split on this spread. The public is paying an inflated price on Seattle at DraftKings (-115).
- A $1.1M sharp wager on NE ML at +188 was placed at Circa; a $2M bet on NE at +200 was placed at Caesars.
- **Edge: ~2-3% implied probability** (you're getting roughly 51.2% implied when sharps suggest ~49%).
- **Best price:** BetMGM/SuperBook at -105 juice on NE +4.5.

#### 2. Under 45.5 Total Points at DraftKings/FanDuel (-105 to -110)
**Why it's +EV:**
- **Circa and Boomer's (sharp books) are already at 45**, a full half-point below DK/FD. This means the sharp market believes the true line is ≤45.
- Getting Under 45.5 at -105/-110 when sharps already moved to 45 is a clear +EV spot.
- SEA allowed 17.1 PPG (1st in NFL); NE allowed 17.3 PPG. Both elite defenses.
- The Under is **5-2 in the last 7 Super Bowls**.
- CBS SportsLine model has Under 45.5 hitting in **59% of simulations**.
- **Edge: ~3-5% implied probability.** You're getting 45.5 when the efficient market says 45.

#### 3. Kenneth Walker III Receiving Yards OVER 20.5 (-110 FanDuel) / OVER 22.5 (-114 DraftKings)
**Why it's +EV:**
- FanDuel has this at **20.5** while DraftKings has it at **22.5**. The FanDuel number is softer.
- Walker has been Darnold's checkdown security blanket in the playoffs: **7 targets, 7 catches, 78 receiving yards across 2 games** (39 yards/game).
- With Zach Charbonnet OUT, Walker will be on the field for virtually every snap, including passing downs.
- NE allowed 5th-most receptions to RBs this season.
- **FanDuel at 20.5 is the +EV play** — sharp projection is ~25-30 receiving yards.

### TIER 2: Strong +EV Plays

#### 4. Hunter Henry OVER 39.5 Receiving Yards (-113 DraftKings / -111 ESPN lines)
**Why it's +EV:**
- Seattle allows ~64 receiving yards/game to TEs (well above league average).
- Seattle primarily plays Cover 3, and Henry leads Patriots in target share vs. zone coverage (21.1%).
- Henry is Maye's pressure release valve — with Seattle's elite pass rush, expect quick throws to the TE.
- Henry OVER 3.5 receptions (-136 DK / -140 at Yahoo) supports the yardage over.
- **Projection: 45-55 receiving yards.** The 39.5 line undervalues the SEA TE weakness.

#### 5. Rashid Shaheed OVER 21.5 Receiving Yards (-112 DraftKings) / OVER 22.5 (-110 FanDuel)
**Why it's +EV:**
- DraftKings line is **21.5** while FanDuel is at **22.5** — DK number is softer here.
- Shaheed has faced mostly zone defenses since joining Seattle, but NE runs man coverage at the **6th-highest rate in the NFL**.
- Shaheed is significantly better vs. man coverage — his speed creates separation.
- NE allows 42.7 receiving yards/game to opposing WR3s.
- CBS SportsLine model specifically flags Shaheed Over 21.5 as a best bet.
- **Play at DraftKings (21.5)** for the extra yard of value.

#### 6. Drake Maye OVER 36.5 Rushing Yards (-102 FanDuel)
**Why it's +EV:**
- FanDuel has this at **-102 juice** (essentially a coin flip), but Maye's rushing data is strong.
- Maye's longest rush line is Over 13.5 yards (-125), suggesting books expect a breakaway.
- In the playoffs: 37-yard rush vs. Chargers, 28-yard rush vs. Broncos.
- Seattle allows rushing lanes when their pass rush collapses the pocket — Maye is mobile.
- **-102 juice implies 50.5% probability; actual probability is closer to 55-58%.**

#### 7. TreVeyon Henderson OVER 18.5 Rushing Yards (-112 DraftKings)
**Why it's +EV:**
- Henderson gets increased work with the Patriots' committee approach.
- Combined rushing + receiving line is 27.5 (-109), suggesting books project ~28+ total yards.
- If they project 28+ total, 18.5 rushing is very achievable as the majority of his touches are carries.
- Multiple expert models flag this as a value over.

- -103 is essentially even money for a prop that projects to hit ~55% in this defensive matchup.

#### 11. Stefon Diggs Fastest Ball Carry Speed UNDER 18.5 mph (-160) [DraftKings]

- -160 implies 61.5% probability; model says 88%. **Massive edge if the model is right.**
